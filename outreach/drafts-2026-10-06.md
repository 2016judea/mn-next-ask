## 3. Upper Midwest GPA chapter, attn. Crystal Nutt
To: uppermidwestgpa@gmail.com

Subject: Cracking the invite-only nut, Nov 4

The Grant Writer Incubator on Nov 4 lists "cracking the 'invite-only' nut." Every 990-PF already answers half of it. It's the Part XV box that says a foundation gives only to preselected organizations.

I read that box on every Minnesota foundation's latest return. 461 of 1,875 left it unchecked. Those 461 gave $911M. And 456 of them filled in how to apply: contact, what to send, deadlines, limits.

That's now a free page called Next Ask. Type a client's name and you see who funds them now, and which open foundations fund their peers but not them yet. No login.

https://mn-next-ask.vercel.app

If it's useful to your members, it's theirs. I'm not selling anything.

Best,
Aidan
