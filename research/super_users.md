# Next Ask: Minnesota super users

Researched 2026-10-06. Pages read with curl or headless Chromium. Nobody was contacted.
Rank = fan-out × how well the four tests hold. Tests: (1) nonprofits already come to them for funder help · (2) they front dozens or more nonprofits · (3) they gain if the tool works · (4) few enough to reach one by one.
Nonprofit counts are IRS EO BMF 501(c)(3) public charities by mailing city, from `data/eo_mn.csv` in this repo.

## 1. Hennepin County Library
- **Fan-out:** 3,482 501(c)(3) public charities have a Minneapolis mailing address (IRS BMF, `data/eo_mn.csv`). The rest of Hennepin County adds more.
- **Tests:** (1) It already pays for Candid, a funder database, with county property-tax money and lists it under "Grants and foundations". (2) Yes. (3) Patrons do better, and Next Ask works away from the building, which Candid at HCL does not. (4) One desk.
- **Contact:** No named grants or nonprofit librarian is published. Use the Ask Us email form https://www.hclib.org/contact/ask-us-email (phone 612-543-5669). Contact page: https://www.hclib.org/contact
- **Their own record (quote for line one):** "Candid Search — Available only in a library. Research grantmakers and nonprofits. Find grants and funders that support individuals or nonprofit organizations." https://www.hclib.org/browse/online-resources?category=Grants-and-foundations
- **Obligation inbox:** Yes. Ask Us exists to answer patrons' questions.
- **Classes:** The BiblioCommons events API returned no grant or funder classes for "grant", "nonprofit", "Candid" or "foundation" through 2026. The closest are Springboard's artist-business sessions, such as "Income Tax Basics for Artists" on 2026-11-14.

- **Contact:** Central Library email centrallibrary@ci.stpaul.mn.us (published on https://sppl.org/locations/c/). Ask a Librarian form: https://sppl.org/ask-a-librarian/ (651-266-7000).

SPPL's news page names her "Workforce and Innovation Center Manager" (May 3, 2022), and an SPPL blog post from Apr 2023 calls her "community services coordinator and an interim public services manager". Her current role is **not verified**, so address the desk and name her only as a cc.
- **Their own record:** "Please ask at the desk in the Workforce and Innovation Center to use Foundation Directory Online. Staff members are always available to assist users with the collection during regular library hours." https://sppl.org/foundation-collection/
  - Also: "Candid … Only available at George Latimer Central Library" https://sppl.org/resource/candid/
- **Obligation inbox:** Yes.

## Fails the tests

- **Minnesota Council of Nonprofits: does not qualify. It is a competitor.** It sells the **2026 Minnesota Grants Directory – PDF Edition, "Member Price: $45 | Nonmember Price: $90"**. The directory covers "over 100 community foundations, corporate giving programs and private foundations" with "2026 applications and inquiry deadlines and application procedures". That is Next Ask's job, done for fewer funders (100+ against 1,875) and behind a paywall. https://minnesotanonprofits.org/resources-tools/publications/2026-minnesota-grants-directory-pdf-edition-pre-order. MCN also runs GrantAdvisor.org. It co-hosts the GPA Grant Writer Incubator, so reach the incubator through the GPA facilitator (block 3), not through MCN.
- **Access Philanthropy: a competitor.** It sells Minnesota funder directories and workshops, e.g. the "2025 National Directory of Top 100+ National Funders in Minnesota" and the MN Arts Funders book, with registration through MCN at $95/$135. https://accessphilanthropy.com/materials-from-past-workshops
- **Minnesota Council on Foundations: not a channel.** Its members are foundations, not grantseekers. Its Nonprofit Resources page promotes a rival free tool: "MCF has launched a three year partnership with Impala to bring premium Impala data services to Minnesota free of charge" (launched October 2024). https://mcf.org/nonprofit-resources (Cloudflare blocks plain requests; it was read in headless Chromium.) No line on the page recommends Candid or a library.
- **Propel Nonprofits:** It is no longer a fiscal sponsor. "The Fiscal Sponsorship Program at Propel will remain active through March 31, 2026 … we are not taking new inquiries." https://propelnonprofits.org/blog/programmatic-update-sunsetting-fiscal-sponsorship-at-propel/. It still trains (finance and board) through SWIF, but no funder-research offering was found.
- **AFP Minnesota:** Its members are mostly in-house fundraisers, so each fronts about one org and test 2 fails. No member count is published.
- **United Way (MN):** No grant-research or capacity program for partner agencies was found.
- **Other MN library systems:** Duluth's databases page lists no Candid. The Rochester, Dakota, Ramsey and Great River research URLs returned 404, and Candid's own locator (candid.org/resources/candid-near-you) blocks both curl and headless requests. No other MN library with Candid access was confirmed. Ramsey County Library does host Springboard's grantwriting workshop (block 5).
- **Independent consultants (the most visible ones; none publishes a client list):** J. Murphy & Associates, Minneapolis, since 2006, 15 people on its team page, julie.murphy@jmurphyassociates.com · Future Funding, Ellen Davis, ellen@futurefundingconsulting.com · AML Grant Services, anne@amlgrantservices.com · The Dotted Line Collaborative, Jan Castillo, jan@dotlinecollab.com. All emails are from their MCN Business Finder listings. Each one alone probably fronts fewer than dozens of nonprofits. Reach them as a group through the GPA chapter (block 3).
- **Regional arts councils** (Region 2, ARAC and others) run grant-writing help, but it is aimed at their own grants. Not checked further.
