    Where this comes from. Every gift listed by Minnesota foundations and grantmakers on their latest IRS tax return (Form 990-PF, or Schedule I of Form 990), mostly tax year : gifts, of them to Minnesota groups. "Takes applications" means the foundation did not check the "gives only to pre-selected groups" box. How to apply, contacts and deadlines are copied from the return's "application information" section (Part XV) — check the foundation's own site before you send anything. Names in tax returns are typed by hand, so one group can appear under several spellings. Gifts from people, companies and the government are not here.
